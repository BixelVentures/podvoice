# Amazon and Google smart displays as references for PodView (iPad wall dashboard)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Several full-text review pages (Tom's Guide, TechRadar, TechSpot) were truncated or blocked (403), so some findings rely on search-result summaries. Aggregator/blog sources are marked as weak.

## Amazon Echo Show 15/21/8/11 and Echo Hub: layout, widgets, ambient, smart home, media, timers, recipes, Alexa+ visuals

### Takeaway
Amazon uses a two-zone landscape home screen: rotating content (photos, ambient visuals, or promoted content) on one side and a widget column/grid on the other, with a separate full-screen smart home dashboard. Widgets are fixed-size and capped (for example, six widgets and six smart home tiles), which reviewers find limiting. Alexa+ (2025) adds person-aware and proximity-aware home screens, conversation follow-ups, chat-bubble transcripts, and tappable recipe steps and timers.

### Cited Findings
- Echo Show 15: up to six Alexa widgets at a time, placed on the right half of the home screen. Two large widgets cannot sit together even when space allows. — [Mixed News](https://mixed-news.com/en/amazon-echo-show-15-review-one-step-forward-two-steps-back/)
- Echo Show 15: widgets cannot be resized, and there are about 17 widgets, some of them only "large" variants of Maps or Calendar. — [Reviewed](https://reviewed.com/smarthome/content/amazon-echo-show-15-review)
- The smart home widget shows only six controls at a time. Gizmodo calls the widgets "wasted potential." Trusted Reviews calls the widget use excellent. — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/amazon-echo-show-15-review-1848457245); [Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/amazon-echo-show-15)
- Visual ID recognises who stands in front of the screen and shows a personalised home screen. The home screen feels cluttered out of the box and gets cleaner once widgets are pruned and profiles are set up. — [Which?/Choose](https://www.choose.co.uk/guide/amazon-echo-show-15/)
- Echo Show 15 2nd gen (updated 20 Nov 2024): auto-framing 13MP camera, plus a proximity sensor that controls brightness and wakes the screen when someone approaches. — [TechRadar 15 vs 21](https://www.techradar.com/home/smart-home/echo-show-15-vs-echo-show-21-is-bigger-always-better)
- Echo Show 21 ($399, 21.5", Fire TV, wall mount and remote included): landscape gets an extra row of widgets, but still only one extra-large widget at the top. Third-party widgets are few and less polished. It weighs over 10 lb. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/smart-home/echo-show-21-review); [T3](https://www.t3.com/home-living/smart-home/amazon-echo-show-21); [iPhone in Canada (Art Mode)](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2025/01/23/echo-show-21-review-art-mode-is-bigger-and-better/)
- Echo Show 8/11 (2025, Alexa+): the home screen shows glanceable info, follow-ups from recent conversations, personalised notifications and topics of interest. Visual ID shows what you usually want as you approach. Ambient visuals change with your distance from the device. — [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/getting-started-echo-show-8-11-alexa-plus-features)
- Amazon help, "Adaptive Content": when you step closer, the screen switches to your favourite widgets. It is listed for Echo Show 8 (2nd gen and newer). — [Amazon help (search summary)](https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/G72DNPRFE2RUHAW7)
- Smart home dashboard (Show 8/11): camera feeds, favourite devices and groups, device status, and one-tap Home/Away/Night modes. It opens from the "more" icon or by voice ("open my smart home dashboard"). A customizable smart home widget and a Tasks widget are listed as "coming soon". A family calendar widget merges calendars with day, week and month views. Media hub: Menu > Music & More (music, ambient sounds, podcasts, books). Ring events can be summarised with footage (Premium). — [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/getting-started-echo-show-8-11-alexa-plus-features)
- Echo Show 8 4th gen: reviewers call Alexa+ and the Omnisense sensor fusion its standout features. TechRadar calls it a solid upgrade, Expert Reviews "better but much the same". Weak source: users complain about ads and the electronic camera shutter. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/smart-home/echo-show-8-4th-gen-review); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/smart-home-hubs/echo-show-8-4th-gen-2025-review); [Expert Reviews](https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/technology/headphones-audio/amazon-echo-show-8-4th-gen-review); [Buyary (weak)](https://buyary.com/reviews/smart-hubs-displays/amazon-echo-show-8-2025)
- Echo Hub (8", wall-mount control panel, $179.99): its proximity sensor brings up the dashboard as you approach. Interactive widgets; the camera widget shows up to six cameras with snapshots. Tom's Guide calls it the most intuitive single place to monitor a home. Expert Reviews says it barely eases mixed-vendor setups. Common cons: weak speaker, chunky body. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/smart-home/amazon-echo-hub-review); [Expert Reviews](https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/smart-speakers/amazon-echo-hub-review); [Verge-score mirror (weak)](https://techyes.pages.dev/24078558/echo-hub-review-alexa-amazon-smart-control-panel)
- Alexa+ visuals: recipe requests give a step-by-step visual layout with tappable timers. Conversations appear as text bubbles, like a messaging thread. The new design looks great on the Show 15 and less so on the small Show 5. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-put-alexa-to-the-test-with-my-chaotic-family-heres-5-things-i-liked-and-2-that-could-use-work); [Tom's Guide 2 weeks](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-spent-the-last-two-weeks-testing-alexa-heres-2-things-that-impressed-me-and-1-that-needs-work)
- Alexa+ visual recognition personalises responses and the homepage per user (Show 8, 10, 15, 21). — [T3](https://www.t3.com/home-living/smart-home/alexa-plus-visual-recognition-feature)
- Criticism: a request for a Ring feed showed a blank screen ("buggy embarrassment"). The 3-month review calls autonomous tasks half-baked. — [Pogue Substack](https://pogueman.substack.com/p/alexa-is-a-buggy-embarrassment); [Tom's Guide 3 months](https://tomsguide.com/ai/ive-been-using-alexa-for-3-months-here-are-my-5-favorite-features-and-one-im-not-a-fan-of)

### Inferences
- The landscape split pattern (ambient or photo area plus a widget column) is the established convention. Reviewer complaints point PodView toward resizable or freely composed tiles and more than six device controls.
- Making smart home a separate dashboard reached by tap or voice (instead of crowding the home screen) is well received, especially one-tap scene modes (Home/Away/Night).
- Interactive timers inside recipe answers are a praised pattern worth copying: the voice answer creates tappable UI.

### Gaps
- No confirmed source for timer or notification banner styling (position, size) on Echo Show. I did not find official Alexa Presentation Language viewport specs (blocked or not searched).
- No source confirming text scaling by distance. Amazon only says "content adapts to proximity".
- Multi-room music UI on Echo Show screens was not documented in the sources found.

## Google Nest Hub / Hub Max with Gemini for Home (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Google keeps the photo-frame ambient mode (Google Photos curation, Ambient EQ dimming) as the default. Gemini for Home (rolled out from Oct 2025) mainly improves voice understanding, with visual upgrades to weather and media answers arriving in 2026. Reception is mixed.

### Cited Findings
- Nest Hub ambient mode automatically picks the best Google Photos shots of chosen people and pets. Ambient EQ adapts the display to room colour and light and dims at bedtime. — [Retailer copy, ox.ee](https://www.ox.ee/en/product/1448434)
- Gemini for Home launched in Oct 2025 alongside new Nest cameras and the Google Home Speaker. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/01/google-reveals-its-gemini-powered-smart-home-lineup-and-ai-strategy)
- The full Gemini experience is on Nest Hub 2nd gen and Hub Max. Older devices get most features but not Gemini Live. — [Google Nest help](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/16618650?hl=en)
- Timers are free. Gemini tracks timers by description ("pause all my bread timers"). Gemini Live and video history search need Google Home Premium. — [Google Store](https://store.google.com/us/magazine/gemini-capabilities-at-home?hl=en-US)
- Google Home 4.18 (early access, 2026) brings detailed on-screen weather visuals (for example, rain at a specific hour), natural-language media search across subscribed services, and conversational news with follow-ups. Continued Conversation keeps the mic open for a few seconds, shown by pulsing lights. — [Gadget Hacks](https://android.gadgethacks.com/news/gemini-for-home-smart-display-update-weather-media-browsing-and-news-features-explained/); [Google "What's new"](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/15962877?hl=en)
- Android Authority says Gemini made an aging Nest Hub and Hub Max better, and that the original Home Hub can now show live camera feeds. T3 reports better Spotify, lighting and broadcast controls, "but people still aren't happy". — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/gemini-google-nest-smart-home-3674394/); [T3](https://www.t3.com/home-living/smart-home/google-upgrades-gemini-for-home-with-better-spotify-lighting-and-broadcast-controls-but-people-still-arent-happy)

### Inferences
- Google's strength is a calm, photo-first ambient screen that adapts to light. It suits a family wall display better than Amazon's busy widget approach.
- Visual answers should carry information the voice cannot carry well (an hourly rain chart, for example), not repeat the spoken text.

### Gaps
- No current (2025–26) source on the Nest Hub "Home View" or controls layout, or on the timer card design. The established layout (swipe up for the Home control panel, top-down quick settings) is background knowledge only and is unsourced here.
- No sources on Google sports or general-answer card layouts under Gemini.

## Proximity and presence-aware UI, font scaling at distance

### Takeaway
Amazon is the only vendor with documented distance-adaptive UI: a far view (ambient, or ads) and a near view (favourite widgets), plus a proximity wake on the Show 15 and Echo Hub. No source specifies font sizes by distance.

### Cited Findings
- Ambient visuals adapt to proximity (Show 8/11 2025). — [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/getting-started-echo-show-8-11-alexa-plus-features)
- Adaptive Content: step closer and the screen switches to favourite widgets. — [Amazon help](https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/G72DNPRFE2RUHAW7)
- Echo Hub shows the dashboard as you approach. Echo Show 15 2nd gen wakes the screen and adjusts brightness via its proximity sensor. — [Verge mirror (weak)](https://techyes.pages.dev/24078558/echo-hub-review-alexa-amazon-smart-control-panel); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/smart-home/echo-show-15-vs-echo-show-21-is-bigger-always-better)
- Reported: when a viewer is more than about 4 ft away, a full-screen ad rotates in between the weather and headlines content. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/audio/smart-speakers/obnoxious-full-screen-ads-are-making-the-amazon-echo-show-useless-heres-how-to-fix-it) (search summary; the article was not fully read)

### Inferences
- An iPad has no proximity sensor. The front camera or a Home Assistant presence sensor could drive a two-state UI (far: big glanceable info; near: dense controls). Font size switching is an inference, not a documented vendor spec.

### Gaps
- No vendor specs for type sizes at distance.

## What reviewers criticise and praise

### Takeaway
The main criticism is ads in the ambient rotation (Echo Show, 2025–26). Other complaints are capped, fixed-size widgets, out-of-box clutter, and Alexa+ bugs. Praise goes to proximity dashboards, camera grids, one-tap modes, photo-frame calm, and interactive recipe timers.

### Cited Findings
- Full-screen ads, often for Alexa+, appear even in personal photo slideshows. Complaints peaked around Oct 2025 and were still reported in Mar 2026. There is reportedly no opt-out, and no discount like ad-supported Kindles get. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/audio/smart-speakers/obnoxious-full-screen-ads-are-making-the-amazon-echo-show-useless-heres-how-to-fix-it); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/109829-echo-show-users-amazon-smart-displays-starting-feel.html); [Gigazine, Mar 2026](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260310-amazon-echo-show-ad)
- Widget limits and fixed sizes (see section 1). — [Reviewed](https://reviewed.com/smarthome/content/amazon-echo-show-15-review); [Tom's Guide 21](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/smart-home/echo-show-21-review)
- Out-of-box clutter. — [Choose](https://www.choose.co.uk/guide/amazon-echo-show-15/)

### Inferences
- PodView's no-ads, family-owned position is a real differentiator. The ambient view should never be hijacked; only household-relevant notifications should interrupt.

### Gaps
- Wirecutter's 2025–26 smart display verdict and The Verge's original ad reporting were not read directly.
